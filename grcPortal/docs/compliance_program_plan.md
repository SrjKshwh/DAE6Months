# Compliance Program Plan for GRC Portal

## Executive Summary
This Compliance Program Plan outlines the comprehensive compliance framework for the GRC Portal, a Governance, Risk, and Compliance management system. The plan establishes organizational roles, responsibilities, and implementation timelines for achieving and maintaining compliance with multiple regulatory frameworks including NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS.

## Organization Overview
**Organization**: GRC Portal Implementation Team
**Business Scenario**: Development and deployment of a web-based GRC management platform for security assessments, risk management, and compliance tracking.
**Applicable Regulations**: NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS

## Organizational Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
**Responsibilities**:
- Overall compliance program oversight and accountability
- Approval of compliance policies and procedures
- Regular reporting to executive management and board
- Coordination with regulatory authorities
- Budget approval for compliance initiatives

**Qualifications**: 5+ years compliance experience, relevant certifications (CISSP, CISA, or equivalent)

### 2. Compliance Manager
**Responsibilities**:
- Day-to-day compliance program management
- Implementation of compliance controls and procedures
- Training program coordination
- Compliance monitoring and testing
- Incident response coordination

**Qualifications**: 3+ years compliance experience, knowledge of relevant frameworks

### 3. Data Protection Officer (DPO)
**Responsibilities**:
- GDPR compliance oversight
- Privacy impact assessments
- Data subject rights management
- Supervisory authority coordination
- Privacy training coordination

**Qualifications**: Privacy law expertise, data protection certifications

### 4. IT Security Manager
**Responsibilities**:
- Technical control implementation
- Security assessments and testing
- Vulnerability management
- Access control administration
- Incident response technical coordination

**Qualifications**: Technical security certifications (CISSP, CEH, or equivalent)

### 5. Risk Manager
**Responsibilities**:
- Risk assessment coordination
- Risk treatment planning
- Risk monitoring and reporting
- Business impact analysis
- Risk appetite definition

**Qualifications**: Risk management experience, quantitative analysis skills

### 6. Compliance Analysts (Team of 3)
**Responsibilities**:
- Compliance monitoring and testing
- Documentation maintenance
- Audit preparation and support
- Control testing execution
- Gap analysis and remediation planning

**Qualifications**: 2+ years compliance or audit experience

### 7. Department Heads
**Responsibilities**:
- Implementation of department-specific compliance requirements
- Staff training coordination
- Compliance incident reporting
- Control ownership and maintenance

## Implementation Timeline

- **Month 1**: Program planning and resource allocation
- **Month 2**: Gap analysis and risk assessment

- **Months 4-5**: Control implementation and testing

**Milestones**:
- 80% of controls implemented
- Initial training completed for all staff
- System go-live readiness

### Phase 3: Optimization (Months 10-12)
- **Months 10-11**: Monitoring and continuous improvement
- **Month 12**: Annual review and audit preparation

**Milestones**:
- Full compliance achieved
- Monitoring systems operational
- Audit readiness confirmed

## Compliance Frameworks and Standards

### Primary Frameworks
1. **NIST SP 800-53**: Security and privacy controls for federal information systems
2. **ISO 27001**: Information security management systems
3. **GDPR**: General Data Protection Regulation (EU)
4. **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
5. **PCI DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

### Implementation Priority
1. NIST SP 800-53 (High priority - government alignment)
2. ISO 27001 (High priority - international standard)
3. GDPR (High priority - data protection)
4. HIPAA (Medium priority - healthcare data)
5. PCI DSS (Medium priority - payment processing)
