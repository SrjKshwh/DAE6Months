## Purpose
This procedure establishes the steps to be taken in the event of a data breach or suspected data breach involving personal data or sensitive information. The goal is to minimize harm to affected individuals, contain the breach, and comply with legal notification requirements.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who handle organizational data. It covers breaches involving:
- Personal data (GDPR)
- Protected health information (HIPAA)
- Payment card data (PCI DSS)
- Confidential business information
- Any other regulated data types

## Definitions

### Data Breach
A breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed.

### Personal Data
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (data subject).

### Incident Response Team (IRT)
Cross-functional team responsible for coordinating breach response activities.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Incident Response Team (IRT) Members
- **IRT Leader**: Chief Compliance Officer or designated representative
- **Technical Lead**: IT Security Manager
- **Legal Counsel**: Organization's legal representative
- **Communications Lead**: Public Relations or Communications Manager
- **Data Protection Officer**: For GDPR-related breaches
- **Business Continuity Coordinator**: For operational impact assessment

### Department Responsibilities
- **IT Security**: Technical containment and investigation
- **Legal**: Regulatory compliance and notification requirements
- **HR**: Employee communication and support
- **Communications**: External stakeholder communication
- **Operations**: Business continuity and recovery

## Breach Response Process

### Phase 1: Detection and Assessment (0-2 Hours)

#### Step 1.1: Breach Detection
- Monitor security alerts, logs, and system notifications
- Employee or third-party reports of suspicious activity
- Automated detection through security monitoring tools
- Customer or partner notifications

#### Step 1.2: Initial Assessment
**Immediate Actions (within 15 minutes):**
1. IRT Leader notified via emergency contact protocol
2. Initial containment measures implemented
3. Evidence preservation initiated
4. Clock started for regulatory notification deadlines

**Assessment Criteria:**
- What data was compromised?
- How many individuals affected?
- What is the potential impact?
- Is the breach ongoing?

#### Step 1.3: Breach Declaration
- IRT convenes within 1 hour of detection
- Breach confirmed based on evidence
- Breach severity determined (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- Response plan activated

### Phase 2: Containment (2-24 Hours)

#### Step 2.1: Immediate Containment
**Technical Actions:**
- Isolate affected systems
- Disable compromised accounts
- Implement emergency access controls
- Deploy additional monitoring

**Communication Actions:**
- Notify key internal stakeholders
- Prepare holding statements
- Activate crisis communication plan

#### Step 2.2: Evidence Preservation
- Secure all relevant logs and data
- Document investigation steps
- Preserve digital evidence for forensic analysis
- Maintain chain of custody

#### Step 2.3: Impact Assessment
- Determine scope and scale of breach
- Identify affected data types and individuals
- Assess potential harm to data subjects
- Evaluate business impact

### Phase 3: Investigation (24-72 Hours)

#### Step 3.1: Forensic Investigation
- Engage forensic experts if needed
- Analyze breach vectors and methods
- Determine root cause
- Document findings

#### Step 3.2: Legal Assessment
- Review notification requirements by jurisdiction
- Assess liability and potential legal actions
- Prepare regulatory filings
- Coordinate with legal counsel

#### Step 3.3: Recovery Planning
- Develop system recovery procedures
- Implement additional security controls
- Plan communication strategy
- Prepare remediation actions

### Phase 4: Notification (24-72 Hours)

#### Step 4.1: Regulatory Notifications
**GDPR Requirements (72 hours):**
- Notify supervisory authority within 72 hours
- Include breach details, consequences, and remediation measures

**HIPAA Requirements (60 days):**
- Notify affected individuals within 60 days
- Notify HHS Office for Civil Rights
- Media notification if >500 individuals affected

**PCI DSS Requirements (24 hours):**
- Notify acquiring bank within 24 hours
- Notify payment brands within 72 hours

#### Step 4.2: Individual Notifications
- Prepare clear, concise notification letters
- Include required information per regulation
- Provide contact information for support
- Offer credit monitoring or other protective services

#### Step 4.3: External Communications
- Notify business partners and vendors
- Prepare media statements if required
- Update website and social media
- Coordinate with PR firm if engaged

### Phase 5: Recovery and Remediation (1-4 Weeks)

#### Step 5.1: System Recovery
- Restore affected systems from clean backups
- Implement enhanced security controls
- Test system functionality
- Monitor for additional compromise

#### Step 5.2: Service Restoration
- Communicate restoration status to users
- Provide support for affected individuals
- Resume normal business operations
- Monitor for secondary impacts

#### Step 5.3: Preventive Measures
- Implement lessons learned
- Update security controls
- Enhance monitoring capabilities
- Conduct additional training

### Phase 6: Post-Incident Review (2-4 Weeks After Breach)

#### Step 6.1: Lessons Learned Review
- Conduct post-mortem analysis
- Identify process improvements
- Update incident response procedures
- Document lessons learned

#### Step 6.2: Regulatory Follow-up
- Respond to regulatory inquiries
- Provide additional requested information
- Implement required corrective actions
- Monitor for enforcement actions

#### Step 6.3: Communication Closure
- Final updates to affected individuals
- Closure communications to stakeholders
- Media follow-up if applicable
- Reputation management activities
