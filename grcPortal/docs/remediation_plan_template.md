## Remediation Planning Framework

### Prioritization Methodology

#### Risk-Based Prioritization
1. **Critical Priority** (Immediate - 0-30 days)
   - Findings with immediate security or compliance risk
   - Regulatory violations with significant penalties
   - Controls protecting critical business processes

2. **High Priority** (Short-term - 30-90 days)
   - Significant deficiencies affecting multiple systems
   - Regulatory requirements with approaching deadlines
   - Controls with high business impact

3. **Medium Priority** (Medium-term - 90-180 days)
   - Moderate deficiencies with limited scope
   - Process improvements for efficiency
   - Documentation and training enhancements

4. **Low Priority** (Long-term - 180+ days)
   - Minor improvements and optimizations
   - Preventive measures and best practices
   - Future state planning

#### Prioritization Criteria Matrix

| Criteria | Weight | Critical | High | Medium | Low |
|----------|--------|----------|------|--------|-----|
| Regulatory Impact | 25% | Major violation | Moderate violation | Minor violation | No violation |
| Security Risk | 25% | Critical vulnerability | High vulnerability | Medium vulnerability | Low vulnerability |
| Business Impact | 20% | Critical process | High impact | Medium impact | Low impact |
| Resource Requirements | 15% | Minimal resources | Moderate resources | Significant resources | Extensive resources |
| Implementation Complexity | 15% | Simple | Moderate | Complex | Very complex |

### Remediation Strategy

#### Approach Options
- **Direct Remediation**: Implement fixes for identified issues
- **Compensating Controls**: Alternative controls to mitigate risks
- **Risk Acceptance**: Documented acceptance of residual risk
- **Process Redesign**: Fundamental process or system changes

#### Success Criteria
- **Effectiveness**: Control operates as intended
- **Sustainability**: Solution addresses root cause
- **Monitoring**: Ongoing validation of effectiveness
- **Documentation**: Proper documentation of changes
