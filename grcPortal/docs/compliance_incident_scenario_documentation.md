## Purpose
This document provides standardized procedures for documenting compliance incidents using established classification, reporting, and follow-up procedures. It ensures consistent incident handling, proper documentation, and effective remediation across all compliance scenarios.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all compliance incidents including data breaches, privacy violations, regulatory non-compliance, security incidents, and ethical violations. It covers incident classification, documentation, reporting, investigation, and follow-up actions.

## Incident Classification Framework

### Primary Classification Categories

#### 1. Data Protection Incidents
- **Data Breach**: Unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of personal data
- **Privacy Violation**: Failure to comply with data protection regulations
- **Consent Issues**: Improper consent management or processing without consent
- **Data Subject Rights**: Failure to honor data subject access, rectification, or erasure requests

#### 2. Security Incidents
- **Unauthorized Access**: Breach of access controls or unauthorized system access
- **Malware Infection**: Virus, ransomware, or other malicious software incidents
- **System Compromise**: Complete or partial system takeover
- **Denial of Service**: Service disruption through attack or overload

#### 3. Regulatory Compliance Incidents
- **Reporting Failures**: Missed regulatory reporting deadlines or incomplete reports
- **Control Failures**: Breakdown in required compliance controls
- **Audit Findings**: Significant findings from regulatory audits or examinations
- **Licensing Issues**: Problems with required licenses, certifications, or permits

#### 4. Operational Compliance Incidents
- **Process Failures**: Breakdown in compliance-required business processes
- **Training Deficiencies**: Failure to provide required compliance training
- **Documentation Issues**: Missing or inadequate compliance documentation
- **Vendor Non-Compliance**: Third-party vendor compliance failures

#### 5. Ethical Compliance Incidents
- **Code of Conduct Violations**: Breach of organizational ethical standards
- **Conflict of Interest**: Undisclosed or improperly managed conflicts
- **Discrimination**: Bias or discriminatory practices
- **Whistleblower Retaliation**: Retribution against good-faith reporting

### Severity Classification

#### Critical (Severity 1)
- **Impact**: Severe regulatory penalties, significant financial loss, criminal liability
- **Scope**: Widespread organizational impact, multiple stakeholders affected
- **Urgency**: Immediate response required, potential business cessation
- **Examples**: Major data breach affecting thousands, critical system compromise

#### High (Severity 2)
- **Impact**: Substantial penalties, material financial impact, reputational damage
- **Scope**: Department or division-wide impact, significant stakeholder groups
- **Urgency**: Response within 24-48 hours, executive management involvement
- **Examples**: Significant privacy violation, regulatory reporting failure

#### Medium (Severity 3)
- **Impact**: Moderate penalties, manageable financial impact, limited reputation damage
- **Scope**: Team or process-specific impact, limited stakeholder groups
- **Urgency**: Response within 1 week, management oversight
- **Examples**: Minor control failure, documentation deficiency

#### Low (Severity 4)
- **Impact**: Minor penalties, negligible financial impact, no reputation damage
- **Scope**: Individual or isolated incident, minimal stakeholder impact
- **Urgency**: Response within 1 month, routine handling
- **Examples**: Training record deficiency, minor documentation issue

## Incident Reporting Process

### Step 1: Incident Detection and Initial Assessment

#### Detection Methods
- **Automated Monitoring**: Security tools, compliance monitoring systems
- **Manual Reporting**: Employee, customer, or third-party notifications
- **Audit Findings**: Internal or external audit discoveries
- **Regulatory Notifications**: Government agency communications
- **System Alerts**: Technical system notifications

#### Initial Assessment Requirements
- **Incident Confirmation**: Verify incident occurrence and basic facts
- **Preliminary Classification**: Assign initial category and severity
- **Scope Determination**: Identify affected systems, data, and individuals
- **Immediate Containment**: Implement temporary protective measures
- **Notification Triggers**: Determine required notification obligations

### Step 2: Incident Documentation

#### Required Documentation Elements

##### Incident Details
- **Incident ID**: Unique identifier (auto-generated)
- **Date/Time Discovered**: When incident was first detected
- **Date/Time Occurred**: When incident actually happened (if known)
- **Reporting Source**: How incident was discovered
- **Location**: Physical or system location of incident

##### Incident Description
- **Summary**: Concise description of what occurred
- **Detailed Description**: Comprehensive account of incident
- **Affected Assets**: Systems, data, processes impacted
- **Affected Individuals**: Number and type of people affected
- **Root Cause**: Preliminary assessment of cause

##### Impact Assessment
- **Business Impact**: Effect on operations and productivity
- **Financial Impact**: Direct and indirect costs
- **Reputational Impact**: Effect on stakeholder trust
- **Regulatory Impact**: Potential compliance violations
- **Legal Impact**: Potential liability or litigation

##### Response Actions
- **Immediate Actions**: Steps taken at discovery
- **Containment Measures**: Actions to prevent spread
- **Investigation Steps**: Preliminary investigation activities
- **Communication Actions**: Notifications made to date

### Step 3: Incident Investigation

#### Investigation Framework

##### Phase 1: Evidence Collection
- **Digital Evidence**: Logs, screenshots, system data
- **Physical Evidence**: Documents, devices, access records
- **Witness Statements**: Interviews with involved parties
- **Chain of Custody**: Proper evidence handling procedures

##### Phase 2: Root Cause Analysis
- **Timeline Development**: Chronological sequence of events
- **Contributing Factors**: Identify all contributing elements
- **Root Cause Identification**: Determine primary cause
- **Impact Analysis**: Assess full scope and consequences

##### Phase 3: Contributing Factor Analysis
- **People Factors**: Training, awareness, human error
- **Process Factors**: Procedure deficiencies, oversight failures
- **Technology Factors**: System vulnerabilities, configuration issues
- **External Factors**: Third-party actions, environmental conditions

##### Phase 4: Recommendation Development
- **Immediate Remediation**: Short-term corrective actions
- **Long-term Prevention**: Systemic improvements
- **Control Enhancements**: Additional safeguards
- **Process Improvements**: Updated procedures and training

### Step 4: Incident Resolution and Follow-up

#### Resolution Requirements
- **Action Plan**: Detailed remediation steps with owners and timelines
- **Implementation Tracking**: Progress monitoring and status updates
- **Effectiveness Testing**: Verification of corrective actions
- **Documentation Updates**: Updated procedures and controls

#### Follow-up Activities
- **Lessons Learned**: Post-incident review and analysis
- **Process Updates**: Incorporation of lessons learned
- **Training Updates**: Additional training requirements
- **Monitoring Enhancements**: Improved detection and monitoring

## Regulatory Notification Requirements

### GDPR Breach Notification (Article 33)
- **Timeline**: Within 72 hours of becoming aware of breach
- **Content**: Description of incident, consequences, remedial measures
- **Recipient**: Supervisory authority in affected jurisdictions
- **Documentation**: Notification confirmation and acknowledgment

### Individual Notification (Article 34)
- **Timeline**: Without undue delay (typically within 72 hours)
- **Content**: Clear language explanation, consequences, protective measures
- **Method**: Direct communication with affected individuals
- **Documentation**: Notification records and delivery confirmations

### HIPAA Breach Notification
- **Timeline**: Within 60 days of discovery
- **Content**: Description, date, consequences, mitigation steps
- **Recipients**: Affected individuals, HHS, media (if >500 affected)
- **Documentation**: Notification logs and regulatory filings

### Other Regulatory Notifications
- **PCI DSS**: Acquiring bank within 24 hours, brands within 72 hours
- **SOX**: Audit committee and external auditors as required
- **State Laws**: Varies by jurisdiction and incident type

## Incident Response Team Structure

### Core Response Team
- **Incident Response Coordinator**: Overall coordination and communication
- **Technical Lead**: Technical investigation and containment
- **Legal Counsel**: Regulatory compliance and legal guidance
- **Communications Lead**: Internal/external communications
- **Business Continuity Coordinator**: Operational impact management

### Extended Team Members
- **Compliance Officer**: Regulatory compliance oversight
- **IT Security**: Technical security response
- **HR Representative**: Employee-related incident handling
- **External Experts**: Legal, forensic, or technical consultants as needed

### Team Responsibilities
- **Coordination**: Unified response and decision-making
- **Communication**: Consistent messaging and stakeholder updates
- **Documentation**: Complete incident record maintenance
- **Escalation**: Appropriate level of management involvement

## Performance Metrics

### Response Time Metrics
- **Detection Time**: Time from occurrence to detection
- **Response Time**: Time from detection to initial response
- **Containment Time**: Time to contain incident spread
- **Resolution Time**: Time to complete remediation

### Quality Metrics
- **Documentation Completeness**: Percentage of required fields completed
- **Investigation Thoroughness**: Depth of root cause analysis
- **Communication Effectiveness**: Stakeholder satisfaction with communications
- **Remediation Effectiveness**: Reduction in similar incidents

### Compliance Metrics
- **Notification Compliance**: Percentage of regulatory deadlines met
- **Reporting Accuracy**: Accuracy of regulatory notifications
- **Follow-up Completion**: Percentage of recommended actions implemented

- **Timeline**: Within 30 days of incident resolution

- **Reporting**: Management reporting on incident trends
