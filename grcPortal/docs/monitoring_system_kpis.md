# Monitoring System Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Overview

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the GRC Portal monitoring system, providing quantitative measures of compliance effectiveness, risk management performance, and operational efficiency.

## KPI Categories

### 1. Compliance Monitoring KPIs

#### Compliance Score Trends
- **Definition:** Average compliance score across all frameworks
- **Target:** ≥85%
- **Measurement:** Monthly calculation from compliance assessments
- **Reporting:** Executive dashboard, monthly compliance reports

#### Control Effectiveness Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of controls rated as effective
- **Target:** ≥90%
- **Measurement:** Quarterly assessment of implemented controls
- **Reporting:** Compliance status reports, audit findings

#### Gap Closure Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of identified compliance gaps resolved within timeframe
- **Target:** ≥80%
- **Measurement:** Monthly tracking of gap remediation progress
- **Reporting:** Compliance dashboard, management reports

#### Regulatory Reporting Timeliness
- **Definition:** Percentage of regulatory reports submitted on time
- **Target:** 100%
- **Measurement:** Monthly review of reporting deadlines
- **Reporting:** Compliance calendar, audit reports

### 2. Risk Management KPIs

#### Risk Assessment Completion Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of planned risk assessments completed
- **Target:** ≥95%
- **Measurement:** Monthly tracking of assessment schedule adherence
- **Reporting:** Risk management dashboard, quarterly reports

#### Risk Mitigation Effectiveness
- **Definition:** Reduction in risk scores after mitigation implementation
- **Target:** ≥70% reduction in high-risk items
- **Measurement:** Pre/post mitigation risk score comparison
- **Reporting:** Risk treatment reports, executive summaries

#### Risk Treatment Timeline Compliance
- **Definition:** Percentage of risk treatments completed within planned timeframe
- **Target:** ≥85%
- **Measurement:** Monthly review of treatment plan progress
- **Reporting:** Risk management reports, escalation notifications

#### Residual Risk Monitoring
- **Definition:** Percentage of mitigated risks with acceptable residual risk levels
- **Target:** ≥90%
- **Measurement:** Quarterly residual risk assessments
- **Reporting:** Risk monitoring dashboard, annual risk reports

### 3. Incident Response KPIs

#### Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- **Definition:** Average time from incident occurrence to detection
- **Target:** <24 hours for high-severity incidents
- **Measurement:** Incident timeline analysis
- **Reporting:** Incident reports, security metrics dashboard

#### Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)
- **Definition:** Average time from detection to resolution
- **Target:** <4 hours for critical incidents, <24 hours for high-severity
- **Measurement:** Incident resolution time tracking
- **Reporting:** Incident management reports, executive dashboard

#### Incident Escalation Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of incidents requiring escalation
- **Target:** <20%
- **Measurement:** Monthly incident escalation analysis
- **Reporting:** Incident management dashboard, trend analysis

#### False Positive Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of alerts that are false positives
- **Target:** <15%
- **Measurement:** Alert validation and classification
- **Reporting:** SOC performance reports, alert management dashboard

### 4. System Performance KPIs

#### System Availability
- **Definition:** Percentage of time the GRC system is operational
- **Target:** ≥99.5%
- **Measurement:** Automated uptime monitoring
- **Reporting:** System health dashboard, SLA reports

#### Data Processing Timeliness
- **Definition:** Percentage of automated processes completing within SLA
- **Target:** ≥98%
- **Measurement:** Process execution time monitoring
- **Reporting:** System performance reports, operations dashboard

#### User Satisfaction Score
- **Definition:** Average user satisfaction rating for system usability
- **Target:** ≥4.0/5.0
- **Measurement:** Quarterly user surveys
- **Reporting:** User experience reports, improvement plans

#### Security Incident Rate
- **Definition:** Number of security incidents per month affecting the GRC system
- **Target:** 0
- **Measurement:** Security event logging and analysis
- **Reporting:** Security reports, incident response plans

### 5. Audit and Assurance KPIs

#### Audit Finding Resolution Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of audit findings resolved within agreed timeframe
- **Target:** ≥90%
- **Measurement:** Monthly tracking of finding remediation
- **Reporting:** Audit management reports, compliance dashboard

#### Evidence Collection Completeness
- **Definition:** Percentage of required evidence successfully collected
- **Target:** ≥95%
- **Measurement:** Assessment and audit evidence verification
- **Reporting:** Compliance reports, audit preparation documents

#### Training Completion Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of required compliance training completed
- **Target:** ≥95%
- **Measurement:** Monthly training completion tracking
- **Reporting:** Training management reports, compliance dashboard

## KPI Calculation Methodology

### Data Sources
- Database queries for compliance and risk data
- System logs for performance and incident data
- User surveys for satisfaction metrics
- External feeds for threat intelligence
- Manual data entry for qualitative assessments

### Calculation Frequency
- Real-time: System availability, active alerts
- Daily: Incident metrics, system performance
- Weekly: Risk assessment progress, training completion
- Monthly: Compliance scores, audit findings
- Quarterly: User satisfaction, comprehensive reviews

### Data Validation
- Automated data quality checks
- Manual review of outlier values
- Cross-reference validation with multiple sources
- Historical trend analysis for anomalies

## Alert Thresholds and Escalation

### Critical Thresholds (Immediate Escalation)
- Compliance score drops below 70%
- System availability below 95%
- Critical security incidents
- Regulatory reporting deadlines missed

### Warning Thresholds (Management Review)
- Compliance score between 70-80%
- Risk mitigation delays >30 days
- Incident response times exceeded
- Training completion below 90%

### Information Thresholds (Monitoring)
- Minor KPI variations
- Trend changes requiring attention
- Process efficiency improvements
- Positive performance indicators
