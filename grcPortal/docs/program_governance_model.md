# Program Governance Model for GRC Portal

## Executive Summary
This Program Governance Model establishes the governance structure, communication strategy, and stakeholder identification framework for the GRC Portal compliance program. The model ensures effective oversight, accountability, and coordination across all compliance activities while maintaining alignment with organizational objectives and regulatory requirements.

## Governance Structure

### 1. Governance Council
**Composition**:
- Chief Executive Officer (Chair)
- Chief Compliance Officer (Executive Sponsor)
- Chief Information Security Officer
- Chief Risk Officer
- Chief Privacy Officer
- Department Heads (IT, Legal, Operations, HR)
- External Audit Representative (Observer)

**Responsibilities**:
- Strategic oversight of compliance program
- Policy approval and major decisions
- Resource allocation approval
- Risk appetite setting
- Annual program review and approval

**Meeting Frequency**: Quarterly
**Decision Authority**: Final approval for all compliance matters

### 2. Compliance Committee
**Composition**:
- Chief Compliance Officer (Chair)
- Data Protection Officer
- IT Security Manager
- Risk Manager
- Compliance Analysts
- Department Compliance Coordinators

**Responsibilities**:
- Operational compliance oversight
- Implementation planning and monitoring
- Issue escalation and resolution
- Compliance reporting and metrics review
- Training program oversight

**Meeting Frequency**: Monthly
**Decision Authority**: Operational compliance decisions

### 3. Technical Implementation Team
**Composition**:
- IT Security Manager (Lead)
- System Administrators
- Security Engineers
- Compliance Analysts
- Development Team Representatives

**Responsibilities**:
- Technical control implementation
- System configuration and testing
- Security monitoring setup
- Incident response procedures
- Technical documentation

**Meeting Frequency**: Bi-weekly
**Decision Authority**: Technical implementation decisions

## Stakeholder Identification and Engagement

### Primary Stakeholders

#### 1. Executive Leadership
**Stakeholders**: CEO, CFO, COO, Board Members
**Interests**: Strategic alignment, regulatory compliance, financial impact
**Engagement Methods**:
- Quarterly governance council meetings
- Annual compliance program reviews
- Executive dashboards and reports
- Board presentations

#### 2. Compliance Team
**Stakeholders**: CCO, Compliance Manager, Analysts, DPO
**Interests**: Program success, resource availability, professional development
**Engagement Methods**:
- Daily stand-ups and weekly team meetings
- Performance reviews and feedback sessions
- Professional development opportunities
- Recognition programs

#### 3. IT and Security Teams
**Stakeholders**: CISOs, IT Managers, Security Engineers
**Interests**: Technical feasibility, system performance, security effectiveness
**Engagement Methods**:
- Technical implementation meetings
- Security awareness sessions
- Incident response drills
- Technology roadmap planning

#### 4. Business Departments
**Stakeholders**: Department Heads, Process Owners, End Users
**Interests**: Operational efficiency, minimal disruption, compliance support
**Engagement Methods**:
- Department compliance briefings
- Process improvement workshops
- User training sessions
- Feedback collection surveys

#### 5. External Parties
**Stakeholders**: Auditors, Regulators, Customers, Vendors
**Interests**: Compliance assurance, transparency, partnership success
**Engagement Methods**:
- Audit coordination meetings
- Regulatory reporting
- Customer compliance communications
- Vendor assessments

## Communication Strategy

### Communication Objectives
- Ensure transparency and accountability
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and support
- Facilitate timely decision-making
- Promote compliance awareness and culture
- Demonstrate regulatory compliance

### Communication Channels

#### 1. Formal Channels
- **Governance Council Meetings**: Strategic decisions and approvals
- **Compliance Committee Meetings**: Operational reviews and planning
- **Department Briefings**: Department-specific updates and requirements
- **Annual Compliance Reports**: Comprehensive program status and achievements

#### 2. Digital Channels
- **Compliance Portal**: Centralized documentation and resources
- **Email Newsletters**: Regular updates and reminders
- **Intranet Site**: Policies, procedures, and training materials
- **Dashboards**: Real-time compliance metrics and status

#### 3. Informal Channels
- **Town Hall Meetings**: Open forums for questions and feedback
- **Working Groups**: Cross-functional collaboration on specific topics
- **One-on-One Meetings**: Individual stakeholder engagement
- **Recognition Programs**: Celebrating compliance achievements

### Communication Protocols

#### Escalation Procedures
1. **Operational Issues**: Report to Compliance Manager within 24 hours
2. **Compliance Violations**: Immediate notification to CCO and legal
3. **Regulatory Changes**: Assessment within 48 hours, communication within 1 week
4. **Critical Incidents**: Immediate executive notification and crisis management

#### Reporting Hierarchy
```
Incident/Issue → Department Coordinator → Compliance Manager → CCO → Governance Council → Executive Leadership
```

#### Information Classification
- **Public**: General compliance updates and achievements
- **Internal**: Operational compliance metrics and procedures
- **Confidential**: Incident details, regulatory findings, legal matters
- **Restricted**: Sensitive risk assessments, audit findings

## Decision-Making Framework

### Decision Levels

#### Strategic Decisions (Governance Council)
- Program direction and priorities
- Major policy approvals
- Resource allocation
- Risk appetite setting
- Regulatory interpretation

#### Operational Decisions (Compliance Committee)
- Implementation planning
- Control testing and validation
- Training program adjustments
- Process improvements
- Issue resolution

#### Tactical Decisions (Department Level)
- Day-to-day operations
- Local procedure adaptations
- Resource utilization
- Minor process changes

### Decision Criteria
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Risk impact assessment
- Resource availability
- Business objectives alignment
- Stakeholder impact analysis

### Approval Workflows

#### Policy Approval Process
1. Draft development by Compliance Team
2. Review by Compliance Committee
3. Legal review and approval
4. Governance Council approval
5. Executive leadership final approval
6. Communication and training

#### Change Management Process
1. Change request submission
2. Impact assessment
3. Risk evaluation
4. Approval routing based on impact level
5. Implementation planning
6. Testing and validation
7. Deployment and monitoring
