## Purpose
This Compliance Risk Assessment Methodology establishes standardized procedures for conducting compliance risk assessments using recognized methodologies. It provides a systematic approach to identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing compliance risks with appropriate treatment recommendations.

## Scope
This methodology applies to all compliance risk assessments across regulatory frameworks including GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOX, and other applicable regulations. It covers organizational compliance risks, control effectiveness, and remediation planning.

## Risk Assessment Framework

### Standard Methodology: NIST SP 800-30
The methodology follows NIST SP 800-30 "Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments" with adaptations for compliance-specific requirements.

#### Step 1: Prepare for Assessment
- **Define Scope**: Identify assessment boundaries and objectives
- **Gather Information**: Collect relevant compliance requirements and organizational data
- **Identify Stakeholders**: Determine assessment participants and reviewers
- **Schedule Activities**: Establish timeline and resource requirements

#### Step 2: Conduct Assessment
- **Identify Threats**: Determine compliance threat sources
- **Identify Vulnerabilities**: Assess weaknesses in compliance controls
- **Determine Likelihood**: Evaluate probability of compliance failures
- **Determine Impact**: Assess consequences of non-compliance
- **Determine Risk**: Calculate overall compliance risk levels

#### Step 3: Communicate Results
- **Document Findings**: Record assessment results and recommendations
- **Share Results**: Communicate with stakeholders
- **Develop Recommendations**: Propose risk treatment strategies

#### Step 4: Maintain Assessment
- **Monitor Changes**: Track changes in risk environment
- **Update Assessment**: Revise assessment as needed
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Implement ongoing risk monitoring

## Compliance Risk Identification

### Risk Identification Categories

#### 1. Regulatory Compliance Risks
- **New Regulations**: Emerging regulatory requirements
- **Regulatory Changes**: Updates to existing regulations
- **Interpretation Changes**: New regulatory guidance or interpretations
- **Jurisdictional Changes**: Changes in applicable jurisdictions

#### 2. Operational Compliance Risks
- **Process Failures**: Inadequate or failed compliance processes
- **Control Weaknesses**: Insufficient compliance controls
- **Resource Constraints**: Lack of personnel, budget, or technology
- **Training Deficiencies**: Inadequate compliance training

#### 3. Technology Compliance Risks
- **System Vulnerabilities**: Technical weaknesses affecting compliance
- **Data Management Issues**: Problems with data handling and storage
- **Integration Problems**: Issues with system interconnections
- **Technology Changes**: Impact of new technology implementations

#### 4. Organizational Compliance Risks
- **Cultural Issues**: Lack of compliance culture or awareness
- **Governance Gaps**: Weak compliance governance structures
- **Communication Problems**: Poor compliance communication
- **Change Management**: Issues with organizational changes

#### 5. External Compliance Risks
- **Third-Party Risks**: Vendor and supplier compliance issues
- **Supply Chain Risks**: Extended enterprise compliance problems
- **Market Changes**: Industry or market-driven compliance changes
- **Geopolitical Risks**: International compliance challenges

## Risk Analysis Framework

### Risk Analysis Components

#### Likelihood Assessment
**Scale**: 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High)

| Score | Description | Criteria |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Very Low | Compliance failure extremely unlikely (<1% probability) |
| 2 | Low | Compliance failure unlikely (1-10% probability) |
| 3 | Moderate | Compliance failure possible (11-50% probability) |
| 4 | High | Compliance failure likely (51-90% probability) |
| 5 | Very High | Compliance failure very likely (>90% probability) |

#### Impact Assessment
**Scale**: 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High)

| Score | Description | Financial Impact | Operational Impact | Reputational Impact |
|-------|-------------|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Very Low | <$10K | Minor disruption | Limited awareness |
| 2 | Low | $10K-$100K | Moderate disruption | Local awareness |
| 3 | Moderate | $100K-$500K | Significant disruption | Regional awareness |
| 4 | High | $500K-$1M | Major disruption | National awareness |
| 5 | Very High | >$1M | Critical disruption | International awareness |

#### Risk Score Calculation
**Formula**: Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

**Risk Level Matrix**:

| Likelihood →<br>Impact ↓ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|-------------------------|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1 (Very Low) | 2 (Low) | 3 (Low) | 4 (Medium) | 5 (Medium) |
| **2** | 2 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 8 (Medium) | 10 (Medium) |
| **3** | 3 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 9 (Medium) | 12 (High) | 15 (High) |
| **4** | 4 (Medium) | 8 (Medium) | 12 (High) | 16 (High) | 20 (Critical) |
| **5** | 5 (Medium) | 10 (Medium) | 15 (High) | 20 (Critical) | 25 (Critical) |

## Risk Evaluation and Prioritization

### Risk Level Definitions

#### Critical Risk (21-25)
- **Immediate Action Required**
- **Timeline**: Address within 30 days
- **Escalation**: Executive management and board notification
- **Resources**: Dedicated task force and external expertise if needed
- **Monitoring**: Daily status updates

#### High Risk (13-20)
- **Priority Remediation Required**
- **Timeline**: Address within 90 days
- **Escalation**: Senior management notification
- **Resources**: Cross-functional team assignment
- **Monitoring**: Weekly status updates

#### Medium Risk (6-12)
- **Planned Remediation Required**
- **Timeline**: Address within 180 days
- **Escalation**: Department management notification
- **Resources**: Department-level resources
- **Monitoring**: Monthly status updates

#### Low Risk (1-5)
- **Monitoring and Periodic Review**
- **Timeline**: Annual review
- **Escalation**: No escalation required
- **Resources**: Normal operations
- **Monitoring**: Annual assessment

## Risk Treatment Strategies

### Treatment Options

#### 1. Risk Acceptance
- **Description**: Acknowledge risk and continue operations
- **Criteria**: Risk level acceptable to organization
- **Requirements**: Documented acceptance rationale
- **Monitoring**: Regular risk level monitoring

#### 2. Risk Mitigation
- **Description**: Implement controls to reduce risk
- **Criteria**: Cost-effective risk reduction possible
- **Requirements**: Control implementation plan
- **Monitoring**: Control effectiveness testing

#### 3. Risk Transfer
- **Description**: Transfer risk to third party
- **Criteria**: Suitable transfer mechanism available
- **Requirements**: Contractual risk transfer agreements
- **Monitoring**: Third-party compliance monitoring

#### 4. Risk Avoidance
- **Description**: Eliminate risk through activity cessation
- **Criteria**: Risk outweighs business benefits
- **Requirements**: Activity discontinuation plan
- **Monitoring**: Risk elimination verification

### Treatment Selection Criteria
1. **Effectiveness**: Ability to reduce risk to acceptable levels
2. **Cost**: Financial impact of treatment implementation
3. **Feasibility**: Practicality of implementation
4. **Timeline**: Time required for implementation
5. **Organizational Impact**: Effect on business operations
