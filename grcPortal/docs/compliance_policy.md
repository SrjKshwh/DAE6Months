## Purpose
This Information Security and Compliance Policy establishes the framework for protecting organizational information assets and ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory requirements. It defines the principles, responsibilities, and procedures necessary to maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information while meeting legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and third-party service providers who access organizational information systems, data, or facilities. It covers all information assets, systems, and processes regardless of location or device type.

## Policy Statement
[Organization Name] is committed to protecting information assets and maintaining compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and standards. Information security and compliance are fundamental to our business operations and are integrated into all organizational processes and decision-making.

## Definitions

### Information Assets
Any data, information, knowledge, or intellectual property that has value to the organization, including but not limited to:
- Customer data and personal information
- Financial records and business data
- Intellectual property and trade secrets
- System configurations and security settings
- Employee personal information

### Information Security
The preservation of confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information through the application of administrative, technical, and physical controls.

### Compliance
Adherence to applicable laws, regulations, standards, and contractual requirements related to information security, data protection, and privacy.

## Principles

### 1. Risk-Based Approach
All security and compliance decisions shall be based on identified risks and their potential impact to the organization.

### 2. Defense in Depth
Multiple layers of security controls shall be implemented to provide comprehensive protection against threats.

### 3. Least Privilege
Access to information and systems shall be granted based on business need and limited to the minimum required privileges.

### 4. Continuous Improvement
Security and compliance programs shall be regularly reviewed, tested, and improved based on lessons learned and changing threats.

### 5. Accountability
All individuals are accountable for complying with this policy and associated procedures.

### 6. Transparency
Security and compliance activities shall be transparent to stakeholders while protecting sensitive information.

- Ultimate accountability for information security and compliance

- Review of significant security incidents and compliance issues

- Development and maintenance of compliance program
- Coordination of regulatory compliance activities
- Compliance monitoring and reporting
- Policy development and approval

### Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Development and implementation of information security program
- Technical security control management
- Security incident response coordination
- Security awareness and training

### Data Protection Officer (DPO)
- GDPR compliance oversight
- Privacy impact assessments

- Privacy training coordination

### Department Heads
- Implementation of security and compliance requirements in their areas
- Employee training and awareness

### All Employees
- Compliance with security policies and procedures

## Information Security Requirements

### Access Control
- Access to information systems shall be controlled based on business need
- User accounts shall be unique and managed through formal processes
- Passwords and authentication mechanisms shall meet minimum standards
- Remote access shall be secured and monitored

### Data Classification and Handling
- Information shall be classified based on sensitivity and regulatory requirements
- Handling procedures shall be defined for each classification level
- Data retention and disposal shall follow approved schedules
- Encryption shall be used for sensitive data in transit and at rest

### Physical Security
- Physical access to facilities and equipment shall be controlled
- Visitor access shall be logged and escorted
- Equipment shall be secured against theft and damage
- Clean desk policies shall be enforced

### Network Security
- Network segmentation shall isolate sensitive systems
- Firewalls and intrusion detection shall be implemented
- Remote access shall use secure protocols
- Network monitoring shall detect anomalous activity

### System Security
- Systems shall be hardened according to security standards
- Security patches shall be applied promptly
- Anti-malware protection shall be maintained
- Backup and recovery procedures shall be tested regularly

### Incident Response
- Security incidents shall be reported immediately
- Incident response procedures shall be documented and tested
- Root cause analysis shall be performed for all incidents
- Lessons learned shall be incorporated into improvements

## Compliance Requirements

### Regulatory Compliance
- Compliance with applicable laws and regulations shall be maintained
- Regular assessments shall identify compliance gaps
- Remediation plans shall address identified deficiencies
- Compliance status shall be reported to management

### Standards and Frameworks
- Applicable security frameworks shall be identified and implemented
- Control objectives shall be defined and measured
- Regular audits shall verify compliance
- Certification requirements shall be met

### Contractual Obligations
- Third-party contracts shall include security and compliance requirements
- Vendor assessments shall evaluate security posture
- Service level agreements shall include security metrics
- Breach notification requirements shall be met

## Risk Management

### Risk Assessment
- Regular risk assessments shall identify threats and vulnerabilities
- Risk ratings shall consider likelihood and impact
- Risk treatment plans shall address high-priority risks
- Residual risk shall be monitored and reported

### Business Continuity
- Business continuity plans shall address information security
- Disaster recovery procedures shall protect critical systems
- Backup systems shall be maintained and tested
- Recovery time objectives shall be met

## Training and Awareness

### Security Training
- All employees shall receive security awareness training
- Role-specific training shall be provided
- Training effectiveness shall be measured
- Refresher training shall be conducted annually

### Compliance Training
- Regulatory compliance training shall be mandatory
- Training records shall be maintained
- Competency assessments shall be performed
- Training gaps shall be addressed

## Monitoring and Auditing

### Continuous Monitoring
- Security controls shall be monitored continuously
- Automated alerts shall detect security events
- Log analysis shall identify suspicious activity
- Performance metrics shall be tracked

### Internal Auditing
- Regular internal audits shall assess compliance
- Audit findings shall be documented and tracked
- Corrective actions shall be implemented
- Audit reports shall be reviewed by management

### External Auditing
- External audits shall be coordinated and supported
- Audit findings shall be addressed promptly
- Corrective action plans shall be developed
- Audit reports shall be reviewed by the Board

## Enforcement

### Non-Compliance Consequences
- Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action
- Disciplinary measures may include verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination
- Third-party non-compliance may result in contract termination
- Legal action may be taken for serious violations

### Exceptions
- Exceptions to this policy must be approved by the CCO
- Exception requests must document business justification and compensating controls
- Exceptions shall be reviewed annually
- Emergency exceptions shall be documented and approved retrospectively

## Policy Review and Maintenance

### Annual Review
- This policy shall be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur
- Reviews shall consider changes in threats, regulations, and business requirements
- Updates shall be approved by the Governance Council
- Revised policies shall be communicated to all affected parties

### Version Control
- All policy versions shall be maintained in the document management system
- Version history shall be tracked and auditable
- Previous versions shall be archived for reference
- Document control procedures shall be followed

## Communication

### Reporting Channels
- Security incidents shall be reported through established channels
