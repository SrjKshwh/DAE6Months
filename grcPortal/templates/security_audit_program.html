Audit Procedures & Guidelines
Planning Phase
  1. Define audit objectives and scope
  2. Assemble audit team with appropriate expertise
  3. Develop audit program and procedures
  4. Schedule fieldwork and resource allocation
  5. Notify auditees and coordinate logistics
Execution Phase
  1. Conduct opening meeting with auditees
  2. Execute audit procedures and tests
  3. Document findings and collect evidence
  4. Perform analytical procedures
  5. Conduct interviews and observations
  6. Prepare working papers
Reporting Phase
  1. Evaluate findings against criteria
  2. Develop recommendations and action plans
  3. Prepare draft audit report
  4. Conduct closing meeting
  5. Issue final audit report
  6. Follow up on corrective actions
Quality Assurance
  • Independent review of work papers
  • Supervisory approval of findings
  • Compliance with professional standards
  • Documentation of audit methodology
  • Peer review and quality control
