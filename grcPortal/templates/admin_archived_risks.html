Page Introduction

This page contains archived risk assessment records that have been moved to long-term storage based on data retention policies. These records preserve historical risk analysis data for compliance, trend analysis, and organizational risk management learning.

Functionality Overview:
  • Archived Risk Records: View risk assessments that have been archived, including asset-threat-vulnerability combinations, risk scores, severity levels, and mitigation status.
  • Risk Scoring System: Maintain historical visibility into risk scoring methodology and severity classifications (Critical, High, Medium, Low) for trend analysis.
  • Asset-Threat Analysis: Preserve records of identified assets, associated threats, and vulnerabilities for historical risk landscape understanding.
  • Status Tracking: Track the status of archived risks to understand how risks were managed and resolved over time.
  • Pagination Navigation: Efficiently browse through large collections of archived risk records with pagination controls.
  • Compliance Preservation: Ensure risk assessment history is maintained for regulatory compliance while optimizing active database performance.
  • Archive Metadata: Track archiving timestamps and reasons to maintain complete audit trails for risk management activities.
