Chain of Custody

Page Introduction

The Chain of Custody module maintains the integrity and traceability of digital evidence throughout the investigation process. It ensures that evidence handling is documented, secure, and legally admissible.

How this page works:
  • Evidence Tracking: Displays all evidence items with their current status and location
  • Add Evidence Modal: Form for registering new evidence with initial custody details
  • Custody Transfers: Record transfers of evidence between custodians
  • Integrity Verification: Hash verification and tamper detection
  • Report Generation: Create comprehensive chain of custody reports
  • Audit Trail: Complete history of all evidence handling activities
