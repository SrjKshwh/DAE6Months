About This Page

The APT Campaign Detail page provides an in-depth view of a specific Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) campaign, including threat actor information, attack techniques, and MITRE ATT&CK framework mappings. This detailed documentation helps security teams understand and respond to sophisticated cyber threats.

Key Functionality:
  • Campaign Overview: Comprehensive details including actor name, target sector, and severity assessment
  • ATT&CK Framework Integration: Map campaign techniques to MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques
  • Indicators of Compromise: Document and display IOCs such as IP addresses, domains, and file hashes
  • Technique Documentation: List and describe the specific techniques used by the threat actor
  • Relevance Assessment: Evaluate how relevant the campaign is to your organization's threat landscape
  • Evidence Management: Add detailed evidence and descriptions for each ATT&CK mapping
