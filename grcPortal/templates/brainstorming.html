About Brainstorming Risk Identification

This page facilitates collaborative risk identification through structured brainstorming sessions. Team members can create and participate in sessions to identify potential risks using various methodologies.

How it works:
  • Create Session: Start a new brainstorming session with a clear objective and duration
  • Join Sessions: Participate in active sessions led by facilitators
  • Structured Approach: Use predefined methodologies for effective risk identification
  • Idea Capture: Document and categorize ideas during the session
  • Risk Conversion: Convert high-priority ideas into formal risk assessments
