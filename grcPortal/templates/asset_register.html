Critical Asset Register

Introduction

The Critical Asset Register is a centralized repository for documenting and managing your organization's most valuable assets. It helps prioritize protection efforts by assessing risk exposure based on asset value, criticality, and vulnerability data.

How it Works
  • Register Assets: Add new assets by providing name, type, value, criticality level, threat exposure score, and vulnerability count.
  • Risk Assessment: The system calculates overall risk exposure (1-5 scale) based on the provided parameters to help prioritize security measures.
  • View Reports: Generate detailed risk exposure reports for individual assets to understand their security posture.
  • Track Criticality: Categorize assets as low, medium, or high criticality to focus resources on the most important systems.
