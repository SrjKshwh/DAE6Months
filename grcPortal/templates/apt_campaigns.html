APT Campaigns

About This Page

The APT Campaigns page serves as a centralized repository for documenting and tracking Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) campaigns. It enables security teams to maintain comprehensive records of sophisticated cyber threats, their tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), and assess their relevance to organizational security.

Key Functionality:
  • Campaign Documentation: Create detailed records of APT campaigns with actor information and objectives
  • Threat Intelligence: Document techniques used, indicators of compromise, and target sectors
  • Risk Assessment: Evaluate campaign severity and relevance to your organization
  • ATT&CK Integration: Link campaigns to detailed MITRE ATT&CK framework mappings
  • Campaign Overview: View all documented campaigns with key metrics and status information
  • Detailed Analysis: Access in-depth campaign details including IOCs and evidence
