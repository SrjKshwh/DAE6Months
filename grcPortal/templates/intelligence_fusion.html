About Intelligence Fusion

Intelligence fusion operations combine data from multiple sources to create comprehensive threat intelligence. This process integrates IoCs, threat actor profiles, campaign data, and external intelligence feeds to provide actionable insights.

Fusion Capabilities:
  • Multi-Source Integration: Combine data from OpenCTI, MISP, threat intelligence feeds, and internal sources
  • Correlation Analysis: Identify relationships between indicators, campaigns, and threat actors
  • Risk Scoring: Generate comprehensive risk assessments from fused intelligence
  • Operational Metrics: Track fusion effectiveness and data quality
