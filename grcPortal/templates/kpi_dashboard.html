Introduction to KPI Monitoring Dashboard
What is the KPI Dashboard?

The KPI Monitoring Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your organization's security posture through key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics. It aggregates data from various security systems to present actionable insights for executives, compliance officers, risk managers, and operations teams.

This dashboard transforms raw security data into meaningful visualizations and trends, enabling data-driven decision making and proactive security management across all levels of your organization.

How Does It Work?
  • Multiple Views: Switch between Executive, Compliance, Risk Management, and Operations dashboards tailored to different audiences
  • Real-time Metrics: View live KPI data with automatic refresh capabilities and trend analysis
  • Visual Analytics: Charts and graphs display compliance scores, risk levels, incident response times, and system availability
  • Detailed Tables: Drill down into specific metrics with comprehensive KPI details and status indicators
  • Alert Integration: Active alerts and notifications are prominently displayed for immediate attention
