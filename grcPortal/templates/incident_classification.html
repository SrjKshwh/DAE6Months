Impact Assessment Scale
  • 1 = Minimal impact on operations
  • 2 = Minor disruption, easily contained
  • 3 = Moderate impact, some business disruption
  • 4 = Major impact, significant business disruption
  • 5 = Severe impact, critical business functions affected
Likelihood Assessment Scale
  • 1 = Very unlikely to occur
  • 2 = Unlikely but possible
  • 3 = Moderate likelihood
  • 4 = Likely to occur
  • 5 = Very likely, almost certain
