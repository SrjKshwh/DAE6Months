About This Page

The Data Retention Settings page allows administrators to manage automated archiving and purging of old records across different database tables. This helps maintain optimal database performance, reduce storage costs, and ensure compliance with data retention policies and regulations.

Key Functionality:
  • Retention Policies: Configure retention periods (30-2555 days) for different data types
  • Automated Archiving: Enable/disable automatic archiving based on retention periods
  • Auto-Purge: Set up automatic deletion of archived records after 10 years
  • Manual Archive: Trigger immediate archiving of all eligible records
  • Archive Monitoring: View statistics and access archived data tables
