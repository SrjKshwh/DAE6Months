Introduction

The Brainstorming Session page facilitates collaborative risk identification and idea generation. It provides a structured environment for teams to contribute risk ideas, concerns, and potential issues in a facilitated session format.

How it Works
  • Session Management: Create and manage brainstorming sessions with defined objectives, duration, and participant lists.
  • Idea Submission: Participants can submit ideas categorized by type (operational, financial, strategic, compliance, technical).
  • Facilitator Controls: Session facilitators can end sessions and convert promising ideas into formal risk assessments.
  • Real-time Collaboration: Active sessions allow continuous idea contribution until the facilitator closes the session.
  • Risk Conversion: Transform brainstormed ideas into structured risk assessments for further analysis.
