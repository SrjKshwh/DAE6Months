About This Page

The Risk Management Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of your organization's risk management posture. It serves as the central hub for monitoring risk metrics, identifying critical issues, and tracking governance activities.

How This Page Works:
  • Key Metrics: Displays total risks, critical risks, escalated items, and approval statuses at a glance
  • Risk Identification Methods: Shows the distribution of risks identified through different methods (brainstorming, checklists, SWOT analysis)
  • Risk Heat Map: Visual representation of risks based on likelihood and impact scores
  • Critical Alerts: Highlights risks requiring immediate attention
  • Governance Tracking: Monitors risk appetite alignment and overall governance effectiveness

Use the refresh button to update data and navigate to the Risk Register for detailed risk management.
