Asset Risk Exposure Report

Introduction

The Asset Risk Exposure Report provides detailed analysis of individual asset vulnerabilities and risk factors. It combines asset data with threat intelligence to generate comprehensive risk assessments.

How it Works
  • Risk Calculation: Analyzes asset value, criticality, threat exposure, and vulnerability count to determine overall risk exposure.
  • Detailed Breakdown: Presents structured risk assessment data in a readable format for security decision-making.
  • Actionable Insights: Helps prioritize remediation efforts based on quantitative risk scores.
  • Historical Tracking: Maintains records of risk assessments for compliance and audit purposes.
