SWOT Risk Analysis

About This Page

The SWOT Risk Analysis page serves as the central hub for managing strategic SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analyses. This tool helps organizations identify strategic risks and opportunities through structured analysis of internal and external factors.

How It Works:
  • Analysis Creation: Create new SWOT analyses for different organizational scopes and areas
  • Strategic Framework: Uses the classic SWOT methodology to categorize factors affecting security posture
  • Risk Integration: Seamlessly converts identified weaknesses and threats into formal risk assessments
  • Progress Tracking: Monitor analysis status from creation through completion
  • Collaborative Tool: Supports multiple analysts and integrates with enterprise risk management
  • Methodology Guide: Built-in guidance on SWOT methodology and risk identification principles

SWOT Methodology
Strategic Analysis:
  • Strengths: Internal positive attributes
  • Weaknesses: Internal negative attributes
  • Opportunities: External positive factors
  • Threats: External negative factors
Risk Identification:
  • Weaknesses → Operational risks
  • Threats → Strategic risks
  • Opportunities → Mitigation strategies
  • Strengths → Risk controls
