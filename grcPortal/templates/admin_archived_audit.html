Page Introduction

This page displays archived audit log records that have been moved to long-term storage based on the system's data retention policies. These records are preserved for compliance and historical analysis purposes.

Functionality Overview:
  • Archived Records View: Browse through audit logs that have been archived according to retention settings, including actions, categories, users, resources, and success status.
  • Pagination: Navigate through large sets of archived records with pagination controls showing current page, total pages, and records per page.
  • Statistics Dashboard: View summary statistics including total archived records, current page information, and pagination details.
  • Record Details: Each archived record shows comprehensive information such as action performed, category (Authentication, Authorization, Administration, etc.), user ID, resource affected, success status, IP address, archive timestamp, and archive reason.
  • Retention Compliance: Ensures audit trails are maintained for regulatory compliance while optimizing database performance by archiving older records.
