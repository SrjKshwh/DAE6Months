Ethical Decision Support

Page Introduction

The Ethical Decision Support page provides a structured framework for making ethically sound decisions in complex security and compliance scenarios. It guides users through ethical principles, stakeholder impact analysis, and decision-making processes to ensure organizational actions align with moral and legal standards.

How this page works:
  • Ethical Framework Overview: Displays core ethical principles (Respect for Persons, Beneficence, Non-Maleficence, Justice & Fairness) with detailed explanations
  • Scenario Templates: Pre-built templates for common ethical scenarios like data privacy, security trade-offs, vendor risk, and AI decision making
  • Ethical Decisions Table: Lists all recorded ethical decisions with scenario type, risk level, decision date, review status, and action buttons
  • Decision Creation Modal: Comprehensive form for documenting new ethical decisions with stakeholder analysis, alternatives, rationale, and implementation plans
  • Interactive Features: Template loading, decision viewing/editing, and principle checkboxes for systematic ethical evaluation
  • Risk Assessment: Color-coded risk levels (Critical=Red, High=Orange, Medium=Blue, Low=Green) for ethical decisions
