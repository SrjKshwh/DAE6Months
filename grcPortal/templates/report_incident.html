About This Page

The Incident Report page allows users to submit security incidents for immediate attention by the incident response team. This critical function ensures that security events are documented and addressed according to established procedures.

How It Works:
  • Incident Submission: Users provide detailed information about security events including what happened, when, and impact
  • Severity Assessment: Classify incidents as Critical, High, Medium, or Low based on business impact
  • Response Prioritization: Critical incidents receive immediate attention within 30 minutes
  • Structured Reporting: Guided form ensures all necessary information is captured for effective response
  • Confidential Handling: All reports are treated as confidential and handled according to security policies
  • Response Tracking: Submitted incidents are tracked through the full incident response lifecycle
