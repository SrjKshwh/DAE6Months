Page Introduction

This page provides access to archived incident records that have been moved to long-term storage according to data retention policies. These records maintain historical incident data for compliance, analysis, and organizational learning purposes.

Functionality Overview:
  • Archived Incidents View: Display resolved or closed incidents that have been archived, including incident titles, status, severity levels, reporting information, and timestamps.
  • Incident Status Tracking: View the lifecycle of incidents from open through contained, eradicated, recovered, and closed states, even after archiving.
  • Severity Classification: Maintain visibility into incident severity levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low) for historical analysis and trend identification.
  • Pagination Support: Navigate through large volumes of archived incident records with efficient pagination controls.
  • Retention Compliance: Preserve incident history for regulatory requirements while optimizing active database performance by archiving older records.
  • Archive Metadata: Track when incidents were archived and the reasons for archiving to maintain audit trails.
