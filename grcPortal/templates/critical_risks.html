Page Introduction

The Critical Risks Assessment page provides a comprehensive view of high-priority risks that pose significant threats to the organization. It focuses on risks with critical or high severity levels that require immediate attention and strategic treatment to prevent potential impacts on business operations, compliance, and security.

How this page works:
  • Risk Overview Cards: Displays key metrics including total critical risks, high-score risks, average risk scores, and risks requiring treatment
  • Risks Table: Lists all critical risks with details such as risk ID, asset, threat, vulnerability, severity, risk score, owner, and available actions
  • Risk Treatment Guidelines: Provides standardized frameworks for risk score thresholds and treatment strategies (Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, Avoid)
  • Interactive Features: Allows viewing detailed risk information and managing risk treatment through direct links to individual risk records
