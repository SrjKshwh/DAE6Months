Page Introduction

This page provides a detailed gap analysis for a specific risk management program, identifying areas where current practices fall short of required standards. It helps organizations prioritize remediation efforts and allocate resources effectively to close identified gaps.

How it works: The page displays a summary of all gaps, including their severity, status, and estimated costs. Users can view detailed gap information in a table format, add new gaps through a modal form, and track progress toward closing identified deficiencies. The analysis covers various categories such as policies, processes, technology, and people, providing a comprehensive view of compliance gaps.
