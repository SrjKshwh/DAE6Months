About This Page

The Risk Indicators page displays key metrics and measurements used to monitor risk levels across your organization. It provides real-time visibility into risk trends and triggers alerts when thresholds are exceeded.

How This Page Works:
  • Indicator Cards: Each card shows current value, target, warning/critical thresholds, and status for individual risk indicators
  • Status Alerts: Visual indicators (green/yellow/red) show whether each indicator is within acceptable ranges
  • Configuration Table: Detailed view of all indicator settings including data sources, thresholds, and monitoring frequency
  • Threshold Monitoring: Automatic alerts when indicators exceed warning or critical thresholds
  • Data Sources: Indicators pull data from various systems and can be customized for your environment

Use this page to monitor risk trends and identify when preventive actions are needed.
