Lessons Learned from Recent Incidents
Incident IR-2024-001: Ransomware Attack
What Went Well
  • Early detection through Wazuh alerts
  • Quick isolation of affected systems
  • Effective communication with stakeholders
  • Successful data recovery from backups
  • Comprehensive documentation maintained
What Could Be Improved
  • Email security controls were insufficient
  • Response team coordination could be faster
  • Automated response actions were limited
  • Stakeholder notification process was manual
  • Post-incident testing was not comprehensive
Root Causes Identified
Primary: Weak email attachment filtering
Contributing: Lack of user training reinforcement
Systemic: Insufficient automated detection capabilities
Improvement Opportunities
Immediate Actions (0-30 days)
  • Deploy advanced email gateway
  • Implement macro blocking
  • Update endpoint protection
  • Enhance backup procedures
Long-term Improvements (30-90 days)
  • Implement SOAR platform
  • Develop automated playbooks
  • Enhance monitoring capabilities
  • Conduct security awareness training
